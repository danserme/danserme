My research informs what I build; my products test what I study. I'm based in Milan, doing applied research at **Politecnico di Milano** and design engineering at **Elysia Solutions**. Over seven years I've shipped across web3, healthcare and emerging tech, and published 10+ peer-reviewed papers along the way.
