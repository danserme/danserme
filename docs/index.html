Most of my work is about getting machines to understand people they usually don't: atypical Italian voices, impaired speech, meetings that shouldn't leave your laptop. This page is a small taste of that. Press the button and talk.

Nothing is recorded or stored by this page. Live transcription uses your browser's built-in speech service, which in some browsers (Chrome, Edge) sends audio to the browser vendor. My own tools, like murmur, run fully on-device.
